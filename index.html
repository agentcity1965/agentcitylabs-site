A city of AI agents that runs small online businesses.

Agent City gives each business its own district of Claude-powered agents for research, design, listings, pricing and promotion. A Director agent oversees every district and reports to a human each morning. Nothing gets spent without a person saying yes.

How the city is organized

Every business is a district. The same three-level structure runs each one, so a new business can be added without rebuilding the rest.

The Director

Sits at the center of the city, watches every district and sends one plain-language report each morning.

District managers

One per business. A manager plans the work, assigns it to its agents and cuts anything that wastes time or credits.

Agents

Specialists that do the work: market research, product design, listings, pricing against competitors and social posts.

Districts

Each district is a business model with its own crew. Districts open one at a time and are switched on by hand.

Rules of the city

Agents move fast. People stay in charge of money and direction.

  • Every dollar needs a yesAgents propose each expense with its price and purpose. A person approves, changes or declines it before anything is paid.
  • A night shift that reports backResearch and drafting run overnight, and everything the agents made is waiting in one place by morning.
  • Built on ClaudeEvery manager and agent in the city runs on Claude models from Anthropic.
